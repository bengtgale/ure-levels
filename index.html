Gauges include the River Ure, its tributaries the Bain and Skell, and the River Ouse (tidal gauges are marked). Levels are metres above each gauge's own datum, so compare a gauge with itself rather than with other gauges. Refreshed automatically every 15 minutes or so (reload the page to see the latest); the Environment Agency gauges report about every 15 minutes.
